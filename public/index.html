About

Hi! I'm Dante and I live in The Netherlands! I've been building stuff since I've been about twelve years old. Even though I had three years of informatica in secondary school, it didn't teach me that much. I started with building Discord bots in Python, discovered Siri Shortcuts around 2019 and I've been doing various things since then. Siri Shortcuts got me interested in building my own websites and that's kind of where I've been stuck. I've done some projects in PHP, which started in 2022. From then on I did some smaller projects that didn't really end up becoming anything. Until 2025 that is, when I begun with Node.js. Since that moment, I have built quite a few projects with it. Most of what I've learnt comes from just trying stuff out! "I have never done it before, so I'm sure I can do it" has been kind of my motto, not just in coding, but also in life.

I work mainly in Node and Express, with PHP, Python and vanilla JavaScript close behind. I self-host my own sites, and I try to make everything I build usable with a keyboard and a screen reader. My area of expertise is Node.js, especially in combination with Express. After that, vanilla JavaScript, Python and PHP are my preferred areas. I try to build my stuff for everyone, therefore I try and choose colour schemes that work for everyone and choose fonts such as Atkinson Hyperlegible.
